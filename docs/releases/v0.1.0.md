First public release. Download **ReelRein-0.1.0-release.apk** below, then follow the [install steps](https://github.com/Naman503/reelrein#install).

### Added
- **Pause before Instagram opens.** A breathing ring, timed on the real clock. It can get 3 s longer with each open today, up to 30 s. Instagram is covered and muted while it's up.
- **Limits you choose.** A drag ruler from 1 to 50 minutes plus quick picks, remembering your last choice. Time's up offers Close Instagram, or a new limit after a 3-second beat.
- **Counter pill.** Time on Reels and reels watched, a limit ring that turns amber in the last minute, and 2× status. Drag it anywhere.
- **Control panel.** Session and today stats, 1× / 2×, Auto scroll, Change limit, Hide pill and End session.
- **Pill customisation.** Choose the items shown, size, style (solid / glass / outline), idle fade, tap action and panel sections. There's a live preview in the app.
- **2× on every reel.** Uses Instagram's own "hold, slide down to lock 2×":
  - waits for Instagram's hint and confirms the lock;
  - tracks each reel separately, so a new reel never inherits the last one's 2×;
  - skips ads, and cancels presses instead of tapping.
- **Auto scroll.** Switches on Instagram's own Auto scroll at the start of each session, reading the real toggle first.
- **Stats.** Today's time on Reels, reels, time in Instagram and opens, plus a 7-day chart.
- **Bank-safe mode.** Optional: accessibility is off whenever Instagram is closed, for banking apps that refuse to run alongside accessibility services.
- **Private.** No internet permission (enforced in CI), daily totals only. It never stores or logs captions, comments, usernames or messages.

### Notes
- 2× and Auto scroll rely on Instagram's English labels (tested on Instagram 450).
- If you were running a build you made yourself, uninstall it once first. The release is signed with a different key.
